# Brief — Chapter 2: How to Play

**Target**: 3000 words (±25%: 2250–3750)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Teach the entire core loop to someone who has never rolled dice for a story.

## Key content
- The roll: 2d6 + Trait, keep the best two, always two.
- Difficulties 6 / 9 / 11 with the names Easy / Tricky / Dazzling.
- The outcome ladder: Miss / Mixed / Hit / Flourish (both kept dice show 6).
- Mixed costs: the Showrunner names one, the player may always substitute
  losing 1 Shine. Table with exact magnitudes.
- Sparkle: starts 3, max 5, what it buys, how it refreshes (1 per scene when
  your Refrain costs you something).
- **Power of Friendship (700 words — the biggest section in the chapter).**
  Backing adds a die and drops one. Backing always costs the backer, with the
  pending-action caveat. Bonds may be spent instead. You cannot back yourself,
  and cannot back someone you have never been honest with.
- Shine and Gloom introduced; Chapter 5 does the detail.

## Design principles
- One complete worked example runs through the middle: a roll, a friend backing
  it at a cost, the dice, the outcome, the consequence.
- Quote the real numbers: 41.7% unbacked, 68.1% with one backer at Tricky.
  Show why backing matters instead of asserting it.

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
