# Brief — Chapter 4: Transformation

**Target**: 2800 words (±25%: 2100–3500)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Deliver the genre promise, and make the Chord feel like something the table
earns rather than a gate that is done to them.

## Key content
- Refracting: crossing into the Spectrum *is* morphing. One act, at the Bridge.
- Solo Morph: 1 Sparkle, grants 2 Shine and raises the cap by 2 while
  transformed, ends when the clock resolves or at the Encore. Once at a time.
  **A Dimmed Star cannot morph.** A pre-problem morph is purely descriptive —
  no cost, no benefit, no duration.
- Giving a morph airtime: the player narrates uninterrupted, nobody rolls.
- Synchronized Morph: the shared command word, 1 Sparkle paid in full by one
  Star, ends with the clock or at the Encore.
- **The Chord**: every Star sounds their note the first time they back someone
  at a cost, have their Refrain cost them, or are pulled back from Dimmed.
- Combined Form: needs a Synchronized Morph, a full Chord, and the Big Finish.
  Best of each Trait **capped at +2**; pooled Shine is the sum of current Shine;
  dissolution splits it evenly, remainder by player choice.
- Finishers: transformed + 1 Sparkle doubles the Gloom removed. The Combined
  Form's finisher empties the clock, paid by any one Star.

## Design principles
- Say plainly that the Chord means the team cannot combine until nobody has
  been left out, and that this will make the table turn toward whoever has been
  quiet. That is the intended effect, not a side effect.

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
