# Brief — Chapter 9: The Gloom

**Target**: 1900 words (±25%: 1425–2375)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Six ready antagonists and a pattern to build more.

## Key content
- How to read a Gloom stat block.
- **Six Glooms**, values verbatim from `npc_registry.md`: The Last Bus (6),
  The Quiet Shelf (4), The Silt (6), The Long Weekend (4), The Grey Ledger (8),
  The Understudy (8).
- Each block: clock size, everyday form, Spectrum body, three moves, and what
  returns to the everyday when it falls.
- Building your own, tied back to Chapter 7's procedure.

## Design principles
- Every Gloom is a real neglect first and a monster second.
- "What returns when it falls" must be concrete and small. The bus runs again.
- The Understudy (burnout) is the one that is about the Stars themselves.
  Handle with care and without cruelty.

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
