# Brief — Chapter 7: Your Town and the Spectrum

**Target**: 2500 words (±25%: 1875–3125)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Hand the setting to the reader and give them a procedure, not a canon.

## Key content
- The everyday: the modern world exactly as it is. No masquerade, no academy,
  no bloodline. Not drab — just unseparated.
- The Spectrum: the same places with the colour unfolded. Full Lisa Frank
  register, written as a place and not as a style note.
- What the Gloom is: what happens when something stops being cared about. It
  wants nothing, and that is the horror.
- **Turning a real problem into a Gloom (650 words — the payload)**: the
  five-step procedure from `concepts/setting.md`.
- Four worked examples, each a real neglect plus its Spectrum body.
- Close on: your town is the real setting; these examples are scaffolding.

## Design principles
- Never call the everyday "the real world."
- Every example must be small and specific. A cancelled bus route beats
  "urban decay."

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
