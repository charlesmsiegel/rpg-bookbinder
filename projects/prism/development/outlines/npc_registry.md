# NPC & Entity Registry

Single source of truth for every value that appears in two or more chapters.

**Rules**
- Any entity with a stat block has an entry here.
- Every chapter referencing an entity uses these values exactly. No approximations.
- Changing a value means changing this file **first**, then every reference.
- Quick Reference tables must match this file exactly.
- **No Trait above +2 appears anywhere**, including here.

## The five pregenerated Stars

Full profiles in Chapter 6. Referenced by Chapter 3 (as worked examples) and
Chapter 10 (the starter Number is written for them).

Every Star uses the fixed spread: one **+2**, two **+1**, one **+0**.
Every Star starts with **Sparkle 3** and **Shine 5**.

### Wren Adeyemi — magenta

- **Canonical Traits**: Heart +2, Flash +1, Craft +1, Cool +0
- **Refrain**: "I don't leave people in the dark."
- **Signature**: The one who goes back. Drops a band on any roll to reach,
  retrieve, or refuse to abandon someone.
- **Radiance**: Magenta and chrome; a coat that trails like a comet.
  Phrase — *"Nobody stays behind."*
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 6
- **Chapters That Reference**: 3, 6, 10

### Tobias "Toby" Lark — cyan

- **Canonical Traits**: Heart +0, Flash +2, Craft +1, Cool +1
- **Refrain**: "Somebody has to be ridiculous first."
- **Signature**: Opening act. Drops a band when doing something publicly,
  loudly, and first.
- **Radiance**: Cyan and holographic; skates that leave a gradient.
  Phrase — *"Watch this bit!"*
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 6
- **Chapters That Reference**: 3, 6, 10

### Priya Raghunathan — sunburst yellow

- **Canonical Traits**: Heart +1, Flash +0, Craft +2, Cool +1
- **Refrain**: "If it's broken I want to know why."
- **Signature**: Takes it apart. Drops a band on understanding how a thing —
  or a Gloom — actually works.
- **Radiance**: Yellow and brass; a coat of working clockwork.
  Phrase — *"Show me the seams."*
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 6
- **Chapters That Reference**: 3, 6, 10

### Marisol Vega — violet

- **Canonical Traits**: Heart +1, Flash +1, Craft +0, Cool +2
- **Refrain**: "I can hold this. Someone has to."
- **Signature**: The steady one. Drops a band on staying calm where calm is

- **Radiance**: Violet and starfield; a stillness the Spectrum bends around.

- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 6
- **Chapters That Reference**: 3, 6, 10

### Danny Okonkwo-Byrne — mint green

- **Canonical Traits**: Heart +2, Flash +0, Craft +1, Cool +1

## Glooms

Full stat blocks in Chapter 9. The Last Bus also appears in Chapter 10.

Stat block shape: **Clock size** / **Everyday form** / **Spectrum body** /
**Moves** / **What returns when it falls**.

### The Last Bus — clock 6

- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 6. Moves: *strand someone*, *make the distance
  longer*, *convince them it was always like this*.
- **Everyday form**: A cancelled night bus route. People stop going out.
- **Spectrum body**: A bus the length of a street, chrome and rust, headlights
  like dead suns, always pulling away.
- **What returns**: The route runs again. Two people who had stopped seeing
  each other are on it.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9
- **Chapters That Reference**: 9, 10

### The Quiet Shelf — clock 4
- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 4. Moves: *shorten the hours*, *lose the thing
  someone needed*, *empty a room*.
- **Everyday form**: A library losing its funding.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9 · **References**: 9

### The Silt — clock 6
- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 6. Moves: *thicken*, *swallow a landmark*,
  *make it somebody else's job*.
- **Everyday form**: A river nobody cleans.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9 · **References**: 9

### The Long Weekend — clock 4
- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 4. Moves: *make it easier to stay in*, *cancel a
  plan*, *offer a good excuse*.
- **Everyday form**: A friend nobody has checked on in a month.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9 · **References**: 9

### The Grey Ledger — clock 8
- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 8. Moves: *reclassify*, *require a form*,
  *promise a review*.
- **Everyday form**: A community centre being quietly wound down.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9 · **References**: 9

### Chapter 7's four sketches

Chapter 7 carries four *additional* Glooms as worked examples of the conversion
procedure. They have no stat blocks and appear nowhere else, so they need no
registry entries — but they must not be confused with the six below:
**The Understair** (4), **Just Us Two** (6), **The Long Grass** (6),
**Vending** (8). Book total: ten Glooms.

### The Understudy — clock 6
- **Canonical Stats**: Clock 6. Moves: *offer to do it for you*, *be better at
  being you*, *ask why you bother*.
- **Changed from 8 to 6 at second draft** (COMMENT-019): it is the most personal
  subject in the book and an 8-clock made it the longest fight as well. Six
  serves the material better.
- **Everyday form**: Burnout — the belief that your effort was never the point.
- **Chapter of Full Profile**: Chapter 9 · **References**: 9
