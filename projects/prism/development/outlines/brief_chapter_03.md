# Brief — Chapter 3: Making a Star

**Target**: 3200 words (±25%: 2400–4000)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
A table with no characters has five in twenty minutes, made together.

## Key content
- The procedure, in fixed order, as a group activity.
- Traits: what each of Heart / Flash / Craft / Cool covers. Fixed spread
  2/1/1/0, no trade, no advancement.
- **Teach the +0 as a creative prompt**: what do you do when the thing in front
  of you is the thing you're worst at? The answer the game wants is "ask
  someone."
- Refrain: a one-line creed in the character's own voice. Examples.
- Signature: drops a task one Difficulty band, or makes something attemptable.
  **Never a bonus to the roll** — say so explicitly.
- Bonds: written aloud, one line per other Star, a specific shared truth.
- Look: untransformed, ordinary, specific.
- Radiance (550 words, the chapter's biggest creative prompt): colour,
  silhouette, sound, the phrase you say, the object you hold.
- Naming the team and inventing the shared command word, together.

## Design principles
- Use the Chapter 6 pregens as worked examples so the two chapters agree.
- Never present the +0 as something to fix.

## Required reading before drafting
- `development/outlines/writing_style.md` — voice, and the binding terminology
- `references/prism/00-core-rules.md` — every number
- `references/prism/01-terminology.md` — every term
- `references/prism/02-probability.md` — every probability
- `development/concepts/premise.md`, `themes.md`, `tone.md`, `setting.md`

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
