# Brief — Chapter 6: Five Stars, Ready to Play

**Target**: 1800 words (±25%: 1350–2250)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Take time-to-play from twenty minutes to zero.

## Key content
- 150 words on how to use these, including "change anything you like."
- **Five complete Stars, ~300 words each**, values taken verbatim from
  `npc_registry.md`: Wren (magenta), Toby (cyan), Priya (yellow), Marisol
  (violet), Danny (mint). Each with Traits, Refrain, Signature, Look, Radiance
  including the phrase, and their Bonds to the others.
- The team's Bond web and the command word: *"PRISM — SPLIT THE LIGHT!"*
  Team name: The Nightline.

## Design principles
- Every value must match `npc_registry.md` exactly. No Trait above +2.
- Bonds must be reciprocal and specific — a shared truth, not a label.
- These five are written to run Chapter 10's Number cold.

## Required reading before drafting
- `development/outlines/writing_style.md` — voice, and the binding terminology
- `references/prism/00-core-rules.md` — every number
- `references/prism/01-terminology.md` — every term
- `references/prism/02-probability.md` — every probability
- `development/concepts/premise.md`, `themes.md`, `tone.md`, `setting.md`

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
