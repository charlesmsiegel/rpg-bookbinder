## Arc

The book teaches a whole game to someone who may never have played one, in the
order a table actually needs it. Chapters 1–3 get you to a made character.
Chapters 4–5 give you the two subsystems that carry the drama. Chapter 6 lets a
table skip creation entirely. Chapter 7 hands the setting back to the reader.
Chapters 8–10 equip the Showrunner and put a playable Number in their hands.

Every chapter is written so a reader who stops there still has something usable.
