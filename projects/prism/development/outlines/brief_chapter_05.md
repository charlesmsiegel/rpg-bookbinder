# Brief — Chapter 5: Trouble

**Target**: 3000 words (±25%: 2250–3750)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
Make conflict legible and make being knocked down feel like an invitation
rather than a punishment.

## Key content
- **Beating a Gloom** (700): clocks start full at 4/6/8; Miss +1 (or **1 Shine
  off the Star if the clock is already full**); Mixed/Hit -1; Flourish -2.
  Empty = win. The clock cannot go below 1 before the Big Finish.
- Clock sizing and setup, with the "what does this neglect cost, and who?"
  question.
- Getting Dimmed: 0 Shine, out of the scene, present, never dead.
- **Reaching someone**: costs the rescuer their action plus 1 Shine or 1
  Sparkle; the Dimmed Star returns with 2 Shine; it sounds their Chord note;
  no roll; it always works.
- Chases, arguments, social pressure — the same roll, different fiction.
- The Encore: full Shine and Sparkle refresh, Bonds return changed.
- **If you run a second Number** (~300 words): the whole of advancement. No
  Trait growth ever. Bonds rewrite, Radiance evolves, Refrain may be rewritten
  once tested, Sparkle maximum +1 every second Number to a cap of 5. Say
  explicitly that this is deliberately not a chapter.

## Design principles
- "The Gloom endures, it does not grow" is the line the whole section turns on.
- A Miss is never free. Say it in those words.

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
