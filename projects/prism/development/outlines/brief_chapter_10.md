# Brief — Chapter 10: The First Number

**Target**: 1700 words (±25%: 1275–2125)
**Outline**: `development/outlines/supplement_outline.md`

## Goals
A Number a first-time Showrunner can run cold, tonight, from the pregens.

## Key content
- **The Last Bus** (1,100): a cancelled night route and the Gloom that fed on
  it. Verse (the everyday, someone stranded), Chorus (first contact goes
  badly), Bridge (refract and morph), Big Finish (the bus, the clock, the
  Combined Form if the Chord fills).
- Quick reference, one page: the roll, difficulties, the ladder, Mixed costs,
  backing, the clock, morph costs.
- Character sheet.

## Design principles
- Written for the five pregens by name, with a line on what each is likely to
  do and which Chord trigger each will most naturally hit.
- The Gloom's stats come from `npc_registry.md` — The Last Bus, clock 6.
- End with the everyday consequence: the route runs again, and two people who
  had stopped seeing each other are on it.

## Required reading before drafting
- `development/outlines/writing_style.md` — voice, and the binding terminology
- `references/prism/00-core-rules.md` — every number
- `references/prism/01-terminology.md` — every term

## Non-negotiables
- No Trait above **+2**. Difficulties are **6 / 9 / 11**.
- `morph` is the verb; `Transformation` the noun; `transformed` the adjective.
  Never "transform" as a verb.
- **Showrunner**, not Gamemaster. **Star**, not player character.
- Every probability comes from `calculate_sum_probability` or
  `references/prism/02-probability.md`. Never estimate.
- Cross-references use anchors from `heading_id_registry.md` only.
- Nothing gates transformation, role, or register on gender; the text never
  remarks on this.
