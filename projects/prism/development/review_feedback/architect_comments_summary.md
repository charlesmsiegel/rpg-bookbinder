# Architect Review — Summary

21 comments across all ten chapters. Full text in
`architect_comments_manifest.md`; markers inline in each `draft_01.md`.

## The four that matter most

**1. Two genuine rules gaps (COMMENT-003, COMMENT-006).**
- *Rerolling with backing dice.* "Reroll one die" is unambiguous on 2d6 and
  ambiguous the moment a friend adds a third. Tables will resolve it
  differently. Needs one explicit sentence.
- *A Signature dropping an Easy task.* There is no band below 6, and this is
  the commonest case at the table. Currently undefined.

Both are small to fix and both would otherwise reach print.

**2. Structural redundancy between Chapters 7 and 9 (COMMENT-015).**
All four worked examples in Chapter 7 reappear as full stat blocks in Chapter 9.
The reader meets each twice. Replacing Chapter 7's four with different subjects
would take the book from six Glooms to ten at no extra word cost — the single
best value change available.

**3. The book's best argument is buried (COMMENT-009).**
"A team cannot combine until nobody has been left out" sits at the end of
Chapter 4. It is the strongest sentence PRISM has and a reader browsing in a
shop would never reach it. It belongs in Chapter 1.

**4. Table size is never addressed (COMMENT-007, COMMENT-014).**
Everything assumes five Stars. Three players changes the Chord materially and
the Bond web from 20 lines to 6. Nothing in the book says so.

## Cross-reference gaps

- Chapter 2 introduces Shine as five boxes; Chapter 4 raises the cap to seven
  with no forward reference (COMMENT-004).
- Chapter 8's Showrunner moves and Chapter 9's per-Gloom moves are two lists
  with no stated precedence (COMMENT-018).
- Chapter 2's "cannot back someone you have never been honest with" has an
  unhandled interaction with Chapter 6's pre-written Bonds (COMMENT-005).

## Continuity

One possible error: Priya's wrench appears in her own Radiance and in Danny's
Bond with different provenance (COMMENT-013). Either deliberate and lovely, or a
mistake; it cannot stay ambiguous.

## Care

The Understudy's safety note sits at the end of its entry, after the reader has
absorbed the concept. It should be first, and clock 8 may be too long a fight
for that subject (COMMENT-019).

## Expansion

Every chapter is below its target and the book is 20,078 of 25,000. Phase 4
should add rather than trim. Chapters 1, 8, and 9 have the most room, and the
comments identify specific content for each.

## Carried from Phase 2

The em-dash problem in `first_draft_findings.md` is unaffected by this review
and still needs the copy-editing pass: 234 book-wide against an allowance of 40.
