# Tone

## Touchstones
- **My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic** — sincerity as the default setting;
  problems solved by understanding someone.
- **Super Sentai / Power Rangers** — the synchronized morph, the colour-coded
  team, the combined form, the pose.
- **Magical-girl anime** — the private transformation, the phrase you say, the
  object you hold, the emotional cost.
- **Barbie (2023)** — bright maximalism played entirely straight.
- **Lisa Frank** — the visual grammar: airbrushed neon rainbow, chrome,
  leopard print, dolphins and unicorns in space, glitter as weather.
- **ABBA and Aqua** — major-key, unembarrassed, enormous chorus. Pop that
  knows exactly what it is and apologizes to nobody.

## What the reader should feel

Welcomed. Slightly overstimulated in a good way. Like they have been handed
something bright and are allowed to be sincere about it in front of their
friends.

## What the reader should never feel

Winked at. The joke is never that this game is silly. No irony quotes, no
"yes, really", no line whose punchline is the genre.

## Colour grammar

The everyday is the world exactly as it is — not drab, just unseparated. The
Spectrum is the same places with the colour unfolded: hyper-saturated,
airbrushed, impossible animals, gradient skies, glitter behaving like weather.
White light already carries every colour; the Spectrum is what you see when
it splits.

## Register check

If a sentence would work in a game that thinks magical girls are funny, cut it.
