# The First Number

## The Last Bus

A complete story for one evening. Written for the five Stars in Chapter 6, hand them out, read this, and run it cold.

**The Gloom:** The Last Bus, clock **6**. Full stats in Chapter 9.

**The hinge, the truth that has to get said:** *"I stopped going out and I
didn't notice."*

**What comes back:** The 41 runs again on Fridays, and two people who had
stopped seeing each other are on it.

---

### Verse, the ordinary world

Open on a Thursday evening in the everyday. No Spectrum, no monster, no hurry.

Go round the five and ask each of them one small question:

- **Wren**: who did you text today and not hear back from?
- **Toby**: what did you nearly break this week?
- **Priya**: what is in your pockets right now?
- **Marisol**: what did you do today that nobody thanked you for?
- **Danny**: who told you something you have not repeated?

Write the answers down. **At least one of them comes back in the Big Finish** —
the person who did not text back is on the bus, the thing Toby nearly broke is
what jams the doors, the thing nobody thanked Marisol for is exactly what she
does again at the worst moment. That is the whole reason you asked.

Then put them somewhere together. A chip shop, a bus shelter, the wall outside
a closed leisure centre. Let them talk for as long as it stays interesting.

**The problem arrives at the end of the Verse.** Keep it small:

> There is a woman at the stop. She has been there a while. She is not agitated
>, she keeps checking the timetable and then checking her phone, and the
> timetable says the 41 comes at 22:47, and it is 23:20.
>
> She says, to nobody: *"It's fine. It was probably always this early."*
>
> And none of you can remember the last time you got the 41.

Let them investigate in the everyday. The timetable is real and says 22:47. The
council page says the route was reduced in March. Nobody remembers a
consultation. The woman's name is Fen and she works late at the bakery on
Tolgate Street.

**No rolls yet unless someone insists.** If they do: Easy.

### Chorus, it goes badly

They find the seam, and they do not go through it. Not yet. Crossing over *is*
morphing, one move and not two ([Chapter 4](#transformation)), and the morph is
the Bridge. What happens in the Chorus is that the Spectrum leans on the
everyday from the other side, hard enough to come through.

A bus shelter at the wrong time of night is the most obvious seam in the book,
so let it be that: the timetable's letters go slightly too crisp, the strip
light takes a beat too long, and Tolgate Street stops being only one street.

**First look, through the seam.** Give this thirty seconds of pure description
and do not let anyone roll through it.

> The chrome shelter reflecting a street with one more shop on it than this one
> has. Glitter banked in the gutters, but only in the reflection. Nine colours
> coming off a puddle that should hold two. Something enormous moving very
> slowly two streets over, and the sound of it arriving half a second after the
> shape does.

Then the bus, which does not stay on its own side of the glass.

> Three storeys of chrome and rust, the length of the whole street, pulling
> away from a stop it never quite reached. Headlights like two dead suns.
> Every window lit and warm and full of people who are not looking out. And on
> the pavement, running, not catching it: Fen.

**Reveal the clock at full — 6.** Say the number out loud and mark all six.

Let them try the sensible thing. It will be one of:

- **Get in front of it.** Flash or Heart, **Tricky**.
- **Get the doors.** Craft, **Tricky**. (Priya's Signature drops this to Easy if
  she works out the hydraulics.)
- **Get Fen.** Heart, **Tricky**. (Wren's Signature drops this, she is
  refusing to abandon someone.)

Whatever they do, it is not enough *against the bus*. If somebody gets to Fen,
they get to Fen: she is off the road, shaken, and safe, and that sticks. What
they cannot do is stop the thing. It does not fight back. It simply keeps
pulling away, and the street keeps getting longer.

**Use the moves:** strand someone, make the distance longer, convince them it
was always like this. On a Miss with the clock full, which it will be, take
**1 Shine** and describe the cold coming off it.

**Nobody should morph here**, which is the same sentence as *nobody should
cross yet*. If someone does anyway, that is your Bridge and you skip to it.

### Bridge, the key change

Make room. Split them up, the bus separates people, that is its whole
function, and put two Stars alone somewhere.

Then ask the question you came for. Aim it at whoever has been quietest:

> *"You haven't been out on a Friday since March. Had you noticed?"*

What you want is somebody saying a version of *"I stopped going out and I
didn't notice."* Wait for it. Do not fill the silence.

When it lands, command word. All five.

> **"PRISM — SPLIT THE LIGHT!"**

And they refract, which is to say they cross and they change in the same
movement, because in PRISM those were never two things.

**Airtime.** Everyone narrates their own morph, uninterrupted, in turn. No
dice. However long it takes.

### Big Finish (transformed)
Turn order starts now: round the table, one action each, you between rounds.

The bus is still pulling away and now they can catch it. Robot unicorns are
absolutely permitted here and Toby will probably suggest it first.

**Run the race.** The clock is wherever the Chorus left it, so 6 if that scene
went badly and 5 if somebody landed a Hit. Everyone's Shine visible. Say the
clock out loud every time it moves.

Things to have happen:

- **Fen**, if nobody got to her in the Chorus, is on the pavement still, and
  going to her costs a whole action and is worth it. If somebody did get to
  her, she is behind them, watching, and will not go inside.
- **The inside of the bus.** If they get aboard: rows of warm, comfortable
  people, none of them looking up, all of them absolutely fine. This is the
  image that makes players angry, in the good way.
- **Round three, roughly**, someone will go Dimmed. Let it happen. Then wait, do not prompt the rescue. Somebody will go.

**Watch the Chord.** Do not announce it when it fills. Let a player notice.

**Combined Form** needs a Synchronized Morph (done at the Bridge), a full
Chord, and the Big Finish (you are in it). When they call it, stop everything
and have all five describe one piece of the fused thing, then weld it together
out loud.

The finisher empties the clock. Let them name it.

### The last thirty seconds

Come back to the everyday. Thursday, 23:40, Tolgate Street, exactly as it was.

> The shelter is just a shelter. There is glitter in the seam of Wren's sleeve
> that will still be there on Sunday.
>
> The timetable says the 41 comes at 22:47, and underneath, on a printed strip
> that was not there before and has clearly been there for years, *Fridays,
> late service, 00:15.*
>
> Fen is sitting down. She is not waiting for anything. She says: *"I might go
> out Friday, actually."*

End there. Encore next session.

## Quick reference

**The roll:** 2d6 + Trait vs Difficulty. Keep the best two dice, always two.

**Difficulties:** Easy **6** · Tricky **9** · Dazzling **11**

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Under | **Miss**: Showrunner makes a move |
| Meets, or +1–2 | **Mixed**: it works, and it costs |
| +3 or more | **Hit**: clean |
| Both kept dice show 6 | **Flourish**: clean, double effect, plus something |

**Mixed costs** (Showrunner names one; player may take 1 Shine instead):
lose 1 Shine · spend 1 Sparkle · Exposure · Gloom +1 tick

**Backing:** +1d6, still keep two. Costs the backer 1 Shine, 1 Sparkle, their
next action (Big Finish only), Exposure, or a Bond.

**Sparkle:** start 3, max 3 (5 only after long play). Morph · reroll a die ·
ignore 1 Shine · Finisher.
Refresh 1 when your Refrain costs you something, once per scene.

**Shine:** 5 boxes. At 0 you are Dimmed, present, not dead, cannot act, cannot
morph. Reaching a Dimmed Star always works, no roll: costs the rescuer 1 Shine
or 1 Sparkle plus their action; they return with 2 Shine; both notes sound.

**Gloom clock:** starts full (4/6/8). Miss +1, or −1 Shine if already full.
Mixed/Hit −1. Flourish −2. Cannot go below 1 before the Big Finish. Empty = win.
Gloom wins only if every Star is Dimmed.

**Signature:** drops a task one Difficulty band. Below Easy there is no band, so
you simply do it, no roll. Never a bonus to the total.

**Morphs:** Solo 1 Sparkle, +2 Shine, cap +2. Synchronized 1 Sparkle paid by one
Star, unlocks Formation / chained backing / group finisher.

**Combined Form** needs all three: a Synchronized Morph, a full Chord, and the
Big Finish. Traits are the best of each across the team, capped at +2. Shine is
the sum of everyone's current Shine. Its finisher empties the clock for 1
Sparkle, paid by any one Star.

**The Chord:** sound your note the first time you back someone at a cost, have
your Refrain cost you, or are pulled back from Dimmed.

**Beats:** Verse (everyday) · Chorus (goes badly) · Bridge (morph) · Big Finish
(transformed) · Encore (between Numbers).
