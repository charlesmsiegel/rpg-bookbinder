# Five Stars, Ready to Play

## How to use these

Pick one. Play tonight.

These five are finished. Traits, Refrain, Signature, Look, Radiance, and every
Bond already written between them. They are built as a team called **The
Nightline**, and [Chapter 10](#the-first-number)'s starter Number is written for exactly these
people.

Everything here is yours to change. Swap a name, move a Trait, rewrite a Bond
that does not sound like you. The only thing worth keeping intact is the Bond
*web*, those lines connect to each other, and pulling one loose leaves a gap
somebody will need in the Big Finish.

If you are using all five, hand them out and read your Refrain aloud in turn.
That is your whole session zero.

Every Star starts with **Sparkle 3** and **Shine 5**.

## Wren Adeyemi (magenta)
**Heart +2 · Flash +1 · Craft +1 · Cool +0**

**Refrain:** *"I don't leave people in the dark."*

**Signature. The one who goes back.** Drops a band on any roll to reach,
retrieve, or refuse to abandon someone.

**Look:** Enormous second-hand parka, sleeves over the hands. Phone at 4%
permanently. Walks fast and keeps checking behind her that everyone is
following.

**Radiance:** Magenta and chrome. A coat that trails behind her like a comet
and throws off sparks that do not go out when they land. Sound: one sustained
chord, low, the kind that makes a room feel bigger. She holds nothing, her
hands stay free, deliberately, because they are for other people.

> *"Nobody stays behind."*

**Transformed:** She always knows the direction of the person who most needs
finding.

**Bonds**
- *To Toby:* You made me laugh at my nan's funeral and I have never told you
  how much that helped.
- *To Priya:* You fixed my bike at midnight and would not let me pay you.
- *To Marisol:* I have seen you hold it together twice and I do not think
  anyone else noticed either time.
- *To Danny:* You asked me twice. Nobody asks twice.

**Playing her:** Wren is the engine. She will get the group moving and she will
go back for people at cost to herself. Cool +0 means she is terrible at being
calm about it, she cannot bluff, cannot wait, cannot pretend it is fine. When
the answer is patience, she needs someone else.

## Tobias "Toby" Lark (cyan)
**Heart +0 · Flash +2 · Craft +1 · Cool +1**

**Refrain:** *"Somebody has to be ridiculous first."*

**Signature. Opening act.** Drops a band when doing something publicly,
loudly, and first.

**Look:** Board under one arm at all times, including indoors. Three jackets in
rotation, all bad. Talks continuously and says very little.

**Radiance:** Cyan and holographic, an oil-slick shimmer that changes with the
angle. Skates that leave a gradient hanging in the air behind him for a second
and a half. Sound: a single wheel spinning down. The board is the object, it
was his brother's, and it still has his brother's stickers on it.

> *"Watch this bit!"*

**Transformed:** He can ride any surface, including ones that are not there.

**Bonds**
- *To Wren:* You never once asked me to calm down.
- *To Priya:* You explained it four times without getting bored and I still
  don't get it, but I liked the fourth time best.
- *To Marisol:* You are the only person who knows I am frightened of it.
- *To Danny:* You heard what I actually said, that time, and not what I said.

**Playing him:** Toby goes first. That is his function and his Signature. Heart
+0 is the interesting part, he cannot do sincerity, it comes out as a joke
every time, and the group needs him to manage it exactly once a session and
watch it be hard.

## Priya Raghunathan (sunburst yellow)
**Heart +1 · Flash +0 · Craft +2 · Cool +1**

**Refrain:** *"If it's broken I want to know why."*

**Signature. Takes it apart.** Drops a band on understanding how a thing, or
a Gloom, actually works.

**Look:** Boiler suit with the sleeves cut off. Pockets full of components that
are not hers. Always faintly covered in something. Reading glasses she denies
needing.

**Radiance:** Sunburst yellow and brass. A coat made of working clockwork, gears
visibly turning at the shoulders and hem, keeping a time that is not quite ours.
Sound: brass settling, a set of small mechanisms finding their places. She
carries a wrench that was her first ever tool and is far too small for anything.
There were two of them in the set. She has never mentioned where the other one
went.

> *"Show me the seams."*

**Transformed:** She can see how anything was assembled, including people,
including Glooms.

**Bonds**
- *To Wren:* You waited outside the exam hall for two hours in the rain.
- *To Toby:* I have watched you fall over eleven times and get up eleven times
  and I think about it more than you would believe.
- *To Marisol:* You said my name right the first time and corrected someone
  else about it later.
- *To Danny:* I told you the real reason and you did not do anything with it.
  You just knew.

**Playing her:** Priya stops to look at the thing. That is the Refrain, and it
will cost the group time at least once a night, which is exactly the point,
because it is also her Sparkle income. Flash +0 means she cannot outrun
anything, ever, and knows it.

## Marisol Vega (violet)
**Heart +1 · Flash +1 · Craft +0 · Cool +2**

**Refrain:** *"I can hold this. Someone has to."*

**Signature. The steady one.** Drops a band on staying calm where calm is the
hard thing.

**Look:** Immaculate. Uniform still ironed at the end of a shift. The stillest
person in any room, which reads as confidence and is not.

**Radiance:** Violet and starfield, not stars printed on cloth but actual
depth, as though the Spectrum thinned out where she stands and let something
enormous through. Sound: almost none. A held breath. She carries her mother's
door keys, and they are the only part of her that makes a noise.

> *"Breathe. I've got the door."*

**Transformed:** Anything she holds shut stays shut.

**Bonds**
- *To Wren:* You noticed. You did not say anything, and you noticed, and that
  was the right order.
- *To Toby:* You are the only thing in my week that is not a responsibility.
- *To Priya:* You asked me a question about me and then waited for the answer.
- *To Danny:* **(unwritten)** — these two have not had the conversation yet.
  Leave this blank until they do. Marisol cannot back Danny, and Danny cannot
  back Marisol, until somebody at the table says the thing out loud. This is
  the honesty rule from [Chapter 2](#how-to-play), deliberately live in your first session.

**Playing her:** Marisol is the one holding the door, literally and otherwise.
Her Refrain will get her hurt, because she will keep saying she can hold it
after she cannot. Craft +0 means she has no idea how anything works and will
not pretend to.

## Danny Okonkwo-Byrne (mint green)
**Heart +2 · Flash +0 · Craft +1 · Cool +1**

**Refrain:** *"You can tell me the bad version."*

**Signature. Hears it.** Drops a band on getting someone to say the true thing
out loud.

**Look:** Cardigan with something in every pocket. Sits down when other people
are standing. Owns an actual paper notebook and writes in it in front of you,
which people find either lovely or unbearable.

**Radiance:** Mint green and pearl, a soft glow with no obvious source that
makes the air around him feel like a small room. Sound: two beats of a song
nobody else can place. No object, he keeps his hands in his pockets, and
somehow that is the most transformed thing about him.

> *"Go on. I'm still here."*

**Transformed:** Anyone who speaks to him tells the truth, and is not hurt by
having done so.

**Bonds**
- *To Wren:* You came back for me when there was no reason to think I was still
  there.
- *To Toby:* I know what the jokes are for.
- *To Priya:* You gave me the wrench. You said it was spare. It was not spare.
- *To Marisol:* **(unwritten)** — see her entry. Write this one at the table.

**Playing him:** Danny gets the truth out of people, which makes him the engine
of half the Bonds and most of the Chord. Flash +0 means he arrives last at
everything. His Refrain will cost him, because people tell him the bad version
and he has to carry it.

## The team's Bonds and command word

**The Nightline.** They named themselves after the bus, which is either
sentimental or a bit much depending on who you ask, and all five of them like
it.

Look at the Bond web above and notice that it is not symmetrical. Marisol has
not told Danny yet. Danny is waiting. Toby's Bonds to everyone are the
lightest, because Heart +0, and his Bond to Marisol is the exception that
proves he can, when it matters.

That asymmetry is the good part. Do not tidy it up.

**Playing with three?** Use Wren, Priya and Danny. That keeps a +2 in Heart,
Craft and Cool, loses Flash entirely (which makes chases genuinely frightening,
and is more interesting than it sounds), and cuts the Bond web to six lines.
Delete the Bonds to Toby and Marisol and leave the rest.

**The command word:**

> **"PRISM — SPLIT THE LIGHT!"**

All five, out loud, at the same time. Practise it once before you start, so the
first time is not the real time.
