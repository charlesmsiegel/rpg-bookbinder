# PRISM Core Rules Reference

Lookup document. Tables and lists, not prose. Authoritative for every number
in the book — if a chapter disagrees with this file, the chapter is wrong.

## The roll

`2d6 + Trait` versus a Difficulty. Keep the best two dice, always.

| Difficulty | Name | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | Anyone could | 6 |
| Tricky | Takes something from you | 9 |
| Dazzling | Should not be possible | 11 |

## Traits

Four: **Heart**, **Flash**, **Craft**, **Cool**. Rated **+0 to +2**.

- Starting spread is fixed: one **+2**, two **+1**, one **+0**.
- No trade, no point-buy, no advancement. **+2 is the permanent ceiling.**
- Maximum ordinary roll: 12 (dice) + 2 (Trait) = **14**.

## Outcome ladder

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Under the Difficulty | **Miss.** The Showrunner makes a move. |
| Meets it, or beats it by 1–2 | **Mixed.** It works, and it costs. |
| Beats it by 3 or more | **Hit.** Clean. |
| Both kept dice show 6, on any success | **Flourish.** Clean, doubles Gloom removed, plus something extra. |

Flourish is double sixes, not a margin. A Hit at Dazzling needs 14 — Trait +2
and both dice sixes — so the ordinary Dazzling success is Mixed.

## Mixed costs

The Showrunner names one. The player may always refuse it and lose 1 Shine instead.

| Cost | Magnitude |
|---|---|
| Shine | Lose 1 |
| Sparkle | Spend 1 (cannot be chosen if the Star has none) |
| Exposure | Something true comes out. No number. |
| Time | The Gloom gains 1 tick |

## Sparkle

- Personal. Starts at **3**, maximum **5** (advancement only).
- **Spend 1** to: power a Solo Morph; reroll one die; ignore 1 Shine loss; fuel a Finisher.
- **Refresh**: when your Refrain costs you something, take back **1 Sparkle**,
  once per scene, never above maximum. Full refresh at an Encore.

## Power of Friendship

- A backer adds **+1d6**. Multiple backers each add one.
- **You always keep exactly two dice.** Every added die also drops one.
- Backing costs the backer: lose 1 Shine, spend 1 Sparkle, give up a
  *genuinely pending* action (carries forward if the scene ends first; not
  available after your last action), or expose something.
- A backer with an unspent **Bond** may spend the Bond instead. A spent Bond
  is marked until the next Encore, where it deepens and returns.
- You cannot back yourself, and cannot back someone you have never been
  honest with.

Verified rates, Trait +1 vs Difficulty 9: **41.7%** / **68.1%** / **82.6%** /
**90.6%** for zero through three backers.

## Shine

- Track of **5** boxes. Restores fully at an Encore.
- At **0** you are **Dimmed**: out of the scene, present, not dead. Never dead.
- **Reaching a Dimmed Star**: takes the rescuer's action and costs them 1 Shine
  or 1 Sparkle. The Dimmed Star returns with **2 Shine**, and being reached
  **sounds their note** on the Chord. No roll. It always works.

## Gloom

- Clock sized **4**, **6**, or **8**. **Starts full.** You win by emptying it.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Miss | Gloom +1 if there is room; **if already full, the Star loses 1 Shine instead**. This is the whole move. |
| Mixed | Gloom −1, and you pay a cost |
| Hit | Gloom −1 |
| Flourish | Gloom −2 |

- **Empties** → you win; the everyday thing comes back.
- **The Gloom wins only if every Star is Dimmed.**
- A Miss's +1 never exceeds the starting size. The Gloom endures; it does not grow.
- **The clock cannot be taken below 1 before the Big Finish.**

## Finishers

- Requires the transformed state. Costs **1 Sparkle**. **Doubles** the Gloom removed.
- The **Combined Form's** finisher **empties the clock outright**. Costs 1 Sparkle
  paid by any single Star, chosen by the players.

## Character elements

1. **Traits** — as above.
2. **Refrain** — one-line creed. Drives Sparkle refresh.
3. **Signature** — **drops a task one Difficulty band** when you are unmistakably
   doing your own thing, or makes attemptable what would otherwise not be rollable.
   **Never a bonus to the roll.** Also names a transformed-state ability.
4. **Bonds** — one written line per other Star. Spendable, finite, return at the Encore.
5. **Look** — untransformed, ordinary, specific.
6. **Radiance** — transformed form: colour, silhouette, sound, phrase, object.

Creation is a group activity, target twenty minutes.

## Transformation

Crossing into the Spectrum is **refracting**, and it is the same act as morphing.

### Solo Morph
- 1 Sparkle. Grants **2 Shine**, raising the cap by the same 2 while transformed.
- **Ends** when the Gloom clock resolves or at the Encore, whichever is first.
  The once-only restriction resets then.
- Only transformed once at a time. No revert-and-remorph to farm the grant.
- **A Dimmed Star cannot morph.**
- Before the problem exists, a morph is **purely descriptive**: no cost, no
  Shine, no Radiance, no duration, and it does not count as having morphed.

### Synchronized Morph
- **1 Sparkle, paid in full by any single Star.** Never split or fractional.
- Unlocks every Star's Radiance plus the team's shared abilities.
- **Ends** when the Gloom clock resolves or at the Encore.

### Combined Form
Requires all three: a Synchronized Morph, a **full Chord**, and the **Big Finish**.

- Trait array: **the best of each Trait across the team, capped at +2.**
- Pooled Shine: **the sum of the team's current Shine**, not their caps.
- On dissolution, divide remaining Shine as evenly as possible; players choose
  the remainder. Anyone on 0 is Dimmed.
- Lasts until the Gloom clock resolves.

### The Chord
Every Star is a note. **You sound your note** the first time in a Number that you:
- back another Star at a cost, or
- have your Refrain cost you something, or
- are pulled back from Dimmed.

One mark per Star. The Chord is full when every note sounds.

## Song structure

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Verse** | The everyday. The problem arrives by the end. |
| **Chorus** | First engagement. It goes badly. |
| **Bridge** | The key change. **Morphing happens here.** |
| **Big Finish** | The climax, fought transformed. |

**Encore** is the between-Numbers downtime scene: Bonds deepen, Sparkle and
Shine refresh fully, advancement is spent.

## Advancement

No Trait advancement, ever. Between Numbers: Bonds rewrite, Radiance evolves,
Refrain may be rewritten once tested, and the **Sparkle maximum rises by 1
after every second Number, hard cap 5**.
